Area of Focus

The timeline has not changed: Abracadabra Sports launches to users in August. The only shift has come from new information that puts a slight hue on product design. Through my research, I’m confident the current leader in the space has made errors in key areas:

  • Offering a baseline tier with marginal value while the premium features sit unused.
  • Misunderstanding that the platform is used primarily for customer acquisition and marketing. The organizational and productivity utilities are more a net cost than a benefit to the user.

Steps to Market Dominance

The goal is still the same—be better. We do that by being more in touch with the user's business, more deliberate, and open as a platform.

What has changed is that the rate of expected organic growth may be much higher than the more optimistic estimates. Mo money mo problems—We need to simplify, refine, and make first contact as soon as able.

  • Focused Engineering Effort – What we’ve got is already better. Everything we build going forward should aim to get new users up to speed and operational on day one.
  • Entry Point – We're ready. The product speaks for itself. The sooner we launch, the faster we can iterate.
