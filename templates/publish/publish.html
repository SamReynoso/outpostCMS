Editorial Control

This page is your audit trail and action center. It is the central interface for managing the publishing workflow of your projects. This is where content changes are tracked, reviewed, and pushed live. Every change you make appears here.

Each project listed below reflects its current status in the publishing pipeline. You’ll see recent changes, unsaved drafts, unsubmitted edits, and visibility settings at a glance.

From here, you can navigate into project-specific views, inspect changes, and move content toward publication. The preview system is tightly integrated, allowing you to review the exact output before finalizing anything. Publishing is done per project, giving you control to stage updates independently.

If you're seeing a large number of entries, remember: each content change is tracked on save and can be reverted. Use this space to ensure your content is ready, clean, and aligned before merging it into your mainline. Git is doing the heavy lifting, but this interface is your command center.
